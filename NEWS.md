# tlapalli 0.0.1

Initial release.

* 27 color palettes, available as both discrete and continuous scales.
* `tlapalli_pal()` returns the colors of any palette.
* Color interpolation to extend a palette to any number of colors.
* Discrete and continuous `fill` and `colour` scales for ggplot2.
* `tlapalli_show()` previews palettes and shows how they look under different color vision deficiencies and in grayscale.
